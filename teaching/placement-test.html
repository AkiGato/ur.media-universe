Before the programme

Where you're starting

Six questions that test whether you can already do what these courses teach, and three about what you want from them. It takes about five minutes and it tells you which course to start with.

What this does with your answers

  • You get your result either way. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you ask for it to be, at the end, with a tick.
  • If you do contribute it: your answers, your score and the time are stored. No name, no email, nothing that identifies you — unless you type it yourself in the last box.
  • Stored answers can be read by anyone who can open this page. Leave the written box blank if that is not acceptable to you.
  • Why it is collected: to find out which ideas are hardest to arrive with, and whether people want this taught at all. That is the only use.
  • You can take the test, read your result and contribute nothing. That is a normal outcome and the button to do it is right beside the other one.

Contributing this result

The six questions test distinctions drawn from Media as Universe: An Antifragile Framework for Communication Design in the Attention Economy and from New Technologies in Storytelling and Gamification for Education and Mental Health. Nothing here is a validated instrument; it is a placement test and it says so.

This page asks before it stores, stores nothing on load, and works the same if you decline. That is the standard the courses teach, applied to their own front door.

# Teaching materials

> **The published copy is the portfolio's, and it is the one that is kept up.**
> These documents are now served from `projects/teaching/` in the portfolio
> repository, where they were restyled into that site's type system
> (Montserrat at one weight, self-hosted), where the placement test was
> rebuilt as a card deck, and where `references.html` was added. Nothing
> copies this folder over that one, and nothing should: edit them there.
> This folder is where they were drafted, kept because that is worth having.
>
> **The kits and the handbooks are not published at all yet.** They sit in
> `facilitator-kits/` and `student-handbooks/` here, in revision; the
> portfolio carries the eight documents that are finished and nothing else.
> Each is self-contained — no document in either folder links to any other —
> so they moved into subfolders without a single path needing to change.


Three four-week studio courses on what communication design does to the people
who receive it. Thirteen self-contained HTML documents — open
[`index.html`](index.html) in a browser and everything else is one click away.

Nothing here is part of the dossier app. The folder has no build step, no
dependencies and no server: every file is a single page that works from the
filesystem.

## The three courses

| | Course | Position | Deliverable |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | **Media as Universe** | What the system does | An artifact, a measured result, a causal analysis |
| 02 | **Human-Centric and Neuroethical Design** | What the designer owes | A design review document and a verdict |
| 03 | **Alternative Storytelling** | What else the mechanics are for | A playable artifact and a mechanic-by-mechanic justification |

Four weeks each, sixteen sessions, 48 contact hours. No prerequisites, and no
course requires another. All three together are a twelve-week sequence.

**Credit values are deliberately absent.** Everything is stated in contact
hours, because the credit value is the host institution's to set against its
own framework.

## Before sending this to anyone

- **Fill in the contact placeholders.** Search the folder for `[Name]` — it
  appears with `[email]` and `[phone]` on the four sheets and on
  `programme.html`.
- **Printing.** The four `sheet-*.html` files are laid out at 210 × 297 mm with
  print styles that drop the screen background and force black on white. Open
  one and press Ctrl+P; there are no margins to adjust and each fits one page.
  The kits and handbooks are screen documents and are not laid out for paper.
- **The placement test records nothing from this folder.** Its storage works
  only in the hosted version. Opened as a local file it scores, explains and
  recommends exactly as normal, and the contribute button reports that it could
  not store and points the taker at *Copy my result* instead. That is the
  intended fallback, not a fault. In the hosted version, only viewers at
  Contributor level or above can write to the record — Viewers, Commenters and
  anyone arriving by public link can take the test but cannot submit it.
- **Handbook drafts stay in the browser.** Anything typed into a working paper
  is kept in that browser's local storage only: never transmitted, not visible
  to the instructor, and cleared when site data is cleared. Each handbook tells
  the student this.
- **Typefaces load from the network.** Newsreader and IBM Plex Sans come from
  Google Fonts. Offline the pages fall back to a serif and a sans and stay
  legible, but the typography is not the intended one.

## Sources

Course one derives from *Media as Universe: An Antifragile Framework for
Communication Design in the Attention Economy* — its §2.1 supplies the pact
argument and its §5.6 the restoration instrument. Course three derives from
Di Patre, P. (2025), *New Technologies in Storytelling and Gamification for
Education and Mental Health*, Pantheon Institute of Design and Technology, and
the three projects it documents: VALLAE, Cloudy Horizons and Coil Hibiki.
Course two is assembled from material both theses already rest on.

Instrument wordings and band thresholds are quoted from the source manuscripts
rather than paraphrased, so a reading taken in one course is comparable with
one taken in another. The one instrument without a manuscript behind it — the
self-determination reading in course three — is assembled from that thesis's
own operational definitions of the three needs, is not a validated scale, and
says so wherever it appears.

## Design

The documents share one system, deliberately: two typefaces at a single weight,
no rounded corners, hairlines instead of card borders, no filled areas, and no
straight segments — every drawn reading is a bowed filament, and its direction
of bow is how the band reads, because the pages are monochrome.

None of them uses an engagement mechanic. No points that persist, no streaks,
no leaderboards, no progress gamification, and no analytics anywhere. That is
not an aesthetic choice; it is the subject of the courses applied to their own
materials, and a student who found one would be right to say so.

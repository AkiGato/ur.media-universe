Antifragile Communication Design

Teaching materials

Three four-week studio courses on what communication design does to the people who receive it. Thirteen documents: a placement test, a programme page, four printable sheets, and a facilitator's kit and student handbook for each course. Everything works offline — open any file in a browser.

Course one — Media as Universe

The attention economy. Students name the mechanics, find where the model breaks, build one thing and measure what it costs.

  • Antifragile Studio Kit

    Facilitator's. Sixteen run sheets, six classroom moves, and four working instruments — the weapons deck, posture roulette, a live restoration reading and the five-question gate.

    kit-1-media-as-universe.html
  • Studio Working Papers

    Student. The terms, six fillable working papers including the delta recording form, and the operational reference.

    handbook-1-media-as-universe.html

Course two — Human-Centric and Neuroethical Design

The designer's obligation. Students judge a shipping product and defend the judgement against a room arguing the other side.

  • Neuroethical Review Kit

    Facilitator's. Run sheets, six adversarial moves including the ignorance defence and the hearing, plus the mechanism deck, vulnerability matrix and regulatory triage.

    kit-2-neuroethical-design.html
  • Design Review Papers

    Student. Six working papers building to the review document, with a 120-word limit enforced on the consent dialogue.

    handbook-2-neuroethical-design.html

Course three — Alternative Storytelling

Narrative and play in education and wellbeing. Taught through three built projects, against three tests.

  • Narrative Studio Kit

    Facilitator's. Run sheets, six moves including the mechanic trial and goal theft, the trial bench, the flow check and the paired reading — plus the three project case studies.

    kit-3-alternative-storytelling.html
  • Pact Working Papers

    Student. Seven working papers, the three tests, the eight-point consent protocol and the reading list.

    handbook-3-alternative-storytelling.html

Before this goes to anyone

  • Fill in the contact placeholders. The four sheets and the programme page carry [Name], [email] and [phone]. Search the folder for [Name] to find all of them.
  • Credit values are deliberately absent. Everything is stated in contact hours, because the credit value is the host institution's to set against its own framework.
  • Printing. The four sheets are built at 210 × 297 mm with print styles that drop the screen ground and force black on white. Open and press Ctrl+P; there are no margins to adjust. The kits and handbooks are screen documents and are not laid out for paper.
  • The placement test records nothing from this folder. Its storage only works in the hosted version; opened as a local file it scores, explains and recommends exactly as normal, and the contribute button reports that it could not store, pointing the taker at Copy my result instead. That is the intended fallback, not a fault.
  • The working papers save drafts to the browser only. Anything typed into a handbook stays in that browser, is never transmitted, and is cleared when site data is cleared. Each handbook says so to the student.
  • Typefaces load from the network. Newsreader and IBM Plex Sans come from Google Fonts. Offline, the pages fall back to a serif and a sans and stay perfectly legible, but the typography will not be the intended one.

Course one derives from Media as Universe: An Antifragile Framework for Communication Design in the Attention Economy. Course three derives from Di Patre, P. (2025), New Technologies in Storytelling and Gamification for Education and Mental Health, Pantheon Institute of Design and Technology, and the three projects it documents. Course two is assembled from material both theses already rest on.

Instrument wordings and band thresholds are quoted from the source manuscripts rather than paraphrased, so a reading taken in one course is comparable with one taken in another.
